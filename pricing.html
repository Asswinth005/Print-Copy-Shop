Pricing FAQ

Questions about cost

Are there any hidden fees?

No. The price we quote is the price you pay. There are no setup fees, file-handling fees, or minimum order charges. Volume discounts are applied automatically and shown on your receipt.

How do bulk discounts work?

Discounts apply automatically based on total page count for copying and printing: 100+ pages gets 15% off, 500+ gets 20% off, and 1000+ gets 25% off. No code needed — it's applied at checkout.

Do you price-match?

If you find a lower published rate at a local competitor for the same service and paper stock, we'll match it. Just bring in their quote — we'll verify and match on the spot.

Is there a minimum order?

No minimum. We're happy to print a single page. That said, bundles and bulk pricing kick in at 100+ pages if you want to save more.

Can I get a formal quote for a large job?

Absolutely. For jobs over 1,000 pages or with complex finishing, we'll prepare a formal written quote with a fixed price and turnaround commitment. Just send us your file and specs.

Do prices include tax?

All prices shown include applicable sales tax for standard retail orders. Business accounts with a valid tax exemption can have tax removed at checkout — just let us know before we process your order.

What happens if I need to cancel or change my order?

If we haven't started printing yet, you can cancel or change your order free of charge. Once printing has begun, we may not be able to refund the cost of materials already used. Talk to us — we'll always try to work something out.

Still have questions?

Our team is happy to talk through your specific job and give you a personalised quote.
