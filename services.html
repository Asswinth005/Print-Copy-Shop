How it works

From file to finished in four steps.

No confusing back-and-forth. Just a clean process that gets your job done right.

Start your order
1

Send your file

Upload online, email us, or walk in with a USB. We accept PDF, DOCX, PPTX, JPG, PNG, AI, and more.

2

We pre-flight it

Our team checks resolution, margins, fonts, and colour space. If something looks off, we flag it before printing.

3

Choose your finish

Pick paper weight, colour mode, binding style, and any finishing extras. We'll confirm the quote before starting.

4

Pick up or delivery

Collect from the shop or have it delivered. Most orders are ready within 30 minutes — you'll get a text when it's done.

Questions

Frequently asked

What file formats do you accept?

We accept PDF, DOCX, PPTX, XLSX, JPG, PNG, TIFF, AI, and Canva share links. PDF is always best for print accuracy, but we can work with almost anything.

How fast can you turn around an order?

Most standard print and copy jobs are ready in 15–30 minutes. Binding typically takes 15 minutes to 4 hours depending on the type. Large-format posters are usually same-day.

Do you offer bulk discounts?

Yes. Discounts are applied automatically at 100+ pages for copying and 250+ pages for printing. For very large runs, contact us for a custom quote.

Can I get a proof before you print the full run?

Absolutely. For orders over 100 pages or any colour-critical job, we'll produce a single proof page for your approval at no extra charge.

Do you keep my files after printing?

No. All customer files are securely deleted from our systems within 7 days of order completion, unless you ask us to keep them for reorders.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept cash, all major debit/credit cards, Apple Pay, Google Pay, and bank transfer for business accounts. Invoices available on request.

Ready to get started?

Send us your file or drop by the shop. We'll take it from there.
