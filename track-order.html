What each status means

Every order moves through the same four stages.

Stage 01

Received

Stage 02

Printing

Stage 03

Binding

Stage 04

Ready

Track order FAQ

Common tracking questions

Where do I find my order number?

Your order number is in the confirmation email we sent when you placed your order. It follows the format PC-XXXX-XXXX. If you paid in person, it's on your printed receipt.

My status hasn't changed in a while — is something wrong?

Probably not. Complex finishing jobs (like hardcover binding) can take a couple of hours to move to the next stage. If it's been more than 4 hours with no movement, give us a call and we'll check on it right away.

Can I change my order after submitting it?

If the status is still "Received," we can usually make changes. Once it's "Printing," changes are difficult. Contact us as soon as possible and we'll do our best.

How long do you hold orders after they're ready?

We hold ready orders for 14 days. After that, we'll reach out once more before recycling the materials. If you need longer, just let us know — we're happy to hold on to them.

Can I track a delivery rather than a pickup?

Yes. If you chose delivery, the status tracker will show "Out for delivery" as the final stage and include an estimated arrival window. You'll get a text from our courier with a live link on delivery day.

The tracker says "Ready" but I haven't received a notification.

Notifications can occasionally be delayed by a few minutes, or land in spam. If the tracker says ready, it's ready — feel free to come collect it. If you're not sure, call us and we'll confirm.
