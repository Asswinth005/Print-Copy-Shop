Before you ask

Common contact questions

How quickly will I hear back?

During opening hours, most enquiries get a reply within an hour. If you send a message overnight or on Sunday, we'll respond first thing the next working day.

Can I get an exact quote without bringing my file in?

Yes — use the form above or email us. If you can tell us page count, paper size, colour mode, binding style, and quantity, we can usually give you a firm price without seeing the file. For complex jobs we may ask to inspect it first.

What's the largest file you can accept?

Our web form accepts files up to 100 MB. For larger files, use a link (Google Drive, Dropbox, WeTransfer) or bring them in on a USB stick. We can handle jobs of any size.

Do you offer trade or business accounts?

Yes. If you're ordering regularly, we can set up a business account with monthly invoicing, a dedicated contact, and preferential rates. Mention it in your message and we'll send over the details.

Is there parking at the shop?

Yes — there are four free spaces directly in front of the shop, plus two hours of free on-street parking right outside. We're also a two-minute walk from the High Street bus stops.

Can I speak to someone rather than email?

Of course. Call us on (555) 742-PRINT during opening hours, or drop by the shop — someone will always be at the counter to help.

Prefer to just talk?

Give us a ring or drop by the shop. Sometimes it's easier to explain what you need in person.
